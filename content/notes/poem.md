*This is my first published poem — a reflection on fear, regret, and finding guidance within ourselves.*

It’s ok to have fear;
It’s ok to not feel ok.
We’ve got big dreams hidden
in the clouds of uncertainty.

But why do our dreams turn into fear?
Because we are in a rush.
Don’t chase—ATTRACT, they say.

Grief harms too.
You cannot run away from it.
Feel it,
but don’t drown in it, they say.

So, how to get out of all these things?
“Get busy,” they say.
“Trust and believe in you. Stay you.
Be you,” they say.

Sometimes you feel the need
for a spirit guide,
someone to hold you back.
“Go to God,” they say.

But where exactly is God?
The universe? The almighty?

“It’s within us,” they say.
Your gut knows exactly what you want,
What you need to do.
Your intuition is your spirit guide, they say—
it leads you to awareness,
to calm,
to knowing.

They who?
It’s You!!
